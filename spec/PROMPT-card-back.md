# 卡背 prompt

卡背會用喺：開卡包時背面出卡、大卡 360° 轉、撳一下翻轉。
畫好之後：轉做 1080×1512，切走四個角（同卡牌一樣嘅切角），再由程式加上**中間嘅鬼臉 icon**同**「損友圖鑑」名**（跟語言轉），所以 AI 圖入面唔要字、中間要留空。

**檢查清單**
- 5:7 直身，上下左右大致對稱
- 四個角 7% 範圍入面冇重要嘢（會被切走）
- 中間有一個空嘅圓形位（大約闊度嘅 40%）
- 冇字、冇數字、冇 logo
- 唔似任何現有卡牌遊戲嘅卡背（冇漩渦、冇波、冇龍）

---

放射線 + 漫畫網點 + 貼紙星星，同網站同卡牌風格最一致。
```
A trading card back design, vertical 5:7 portrait, perfectly symmetrical top to bottom and left to right, flat illustration with bold clean black outlines and smooth cel shading, vibrant colors.
A deep night-blue to violet background with soft rays bursting from the centre, a subtle comic halftone dot pattern, small yellow four-pointed sparkles and tiny orange star stickers spread evenly around the edges.
A thick orange-red outer border with a thin bright yellow inner line, following the card edge.
In the exact centre, an empty plain round medallion about 40% of the card width, filled with a soft cream glow and a thin yellow ring, with nothing inside it.
Nothing important in the four corners. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no faces, no characters, no creatures, no swirls, no balls.
```
