# 標題字（Logo）prompt

用喺首頁大標題（之後亦可以放頂部列）。每個語言各一張，因為名唔同：
- 繁體：損友圖鑑
- 简体：损友图鉴
- 英文：Frenemy Cards

**檢查清單**
- 透明背景（或者純白背景，我會幫你去底）
- 橫身，大約 3:1
- 字要逐隻對：AI 成日寫錯中文字（多筆、少筆、似字非字），要放大睇清楚
- 唔似任何現有卡牌遊戲或者卡通嘅 logo（例如唔好黃字藍邊）
- 細細個都睇得清（手機頂部大約 30px 高）

---

## A：貼紙漫畫風（建議，同卡牌最夾）
```
A bold game title logo that reads exactly "損友圖鑑" (four Traditional Chinese characters, written correctly), wide horizontal layout about 3:1, on a plain transparent background.
Chunky rounded hand-lettered characters, each character slightly tilted and bouncing at a different height like stickers, thick dark brown outline (#2b2118) around every character, a white sticker border around the whole word, and a hard dark drop shadow.
Fill colours: warm orange-red (#ff5a36) fading to sunny yellow (#ffd23f), with a small glossy highlight on each character.
A few tiny comic accents around the word: a small speech bubble, a sweat drop, two little sparkles and short motion lines.
Flat illustration with clean cel shading, playful and cheeky mood. Nothing else in the image: no extra text, no letters, no characters, no mascot, no background.
```

## B：手寫塗鴉風（更搞笑、更「損」）
```
A cheeky game title logo that reads exactly "損友圖鑑" (four Traditional Chinese characters, written correctly), wide horizontal layout about 3:1, on a plain transparent background.
Messy bold marker-pen lettering like a prank note scribbled on a school notebook, uneven letter sizes, thick black strokes, a quick yellow highlighter swipe behind the word, a small doodle devil horn on top of one character and a little cheeky tongue-out face doodle at the end.
Colours: black ink, highlighter yellow (#ffd23f) and one accent of orange-red (#ff5a36).
Fun, naughty, hand-made feeling. Nothing else in the image: no extra text, no letters, no characters, no background.
```

## 其他語言
將 prompt 入面嘅字換做：
- 简体：`reads exactly "损友图鉴" (four Simplified Chinese characters, written correctly)`
- 英文：`reads exactly "Frenemy Cards" (two English words, spelled correctly)`

三張最好用同一個風格、同一次對話入面整，等佢哋睇落係一套。
