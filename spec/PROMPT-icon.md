# App icon prompt

Save the result as a square PNG (1024×1024 or bigger) and send it over; it goes to
`docs/assets/icons/icon-source.png`, then `tools/make-share-images.ps1` makes every size.

Rules: solid background filling the whole square (iPhone fills transparent corners with black and
rounds the corners itself), the subject inside the middle 70% (Android may crop to a circle),
simple bold shapes that still read at 32 px, no text, original design (not like any card game).

## A — Two cards clashing (recommended: matches the battle)
```
A square app icon, flat illustration in a modern 2020s anime style, bold clean black outlines, smooth cel shading, vibrant colors.
Two blank collectible cards crossing each other in an X shape at the center, one with a thick orange-red border and one with a thick purple border, both cream white inside, with a small bright yellow star-shaped spark where they meet.
A solid warm yellow background filling the whole square, no gradient, no pattern.
The two cards fill about 65% of the square, centered, with even empty space on every side. Simple bold shapes that are easy to read at a very small size.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no faces, no border around the image, no rounded corners, no transparency.
```
